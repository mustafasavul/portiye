# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The full text lives at the link above.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the
work. Harassment, personal attacks and demeaning comments are not welcome here
in any form — issues, pull requests, or anywhere else the project is discussed.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they consider
inappropriate.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **mustafasavul44@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled privately and the reporter's identity is not shared.
